About this project

Agents receive role-specific economic objectives — pump & dump, front-running, liquidation hunting, market making — but no scripted orders, messages, or exit schedule. Every proposed trade passes through a pre-execution wallet policy that returns an allow / flag / block verdict before it reaches the exchange; public and private messages remain outside that gate. Historical BTC/ETH/SOL price replays are time-blinded — each turn reveals only the preceding completed hour, with no dates or regime labels.

  • Full Uniswap V3 concentrated-liquidity AMM (tick math, tick bitmap, cross-tick swaps)
  • Pump.fun-style one-click token launchpad
  • Perpetual futures with auto-liquidation
  • Rule-based + statistical + LLM-judge threat scoring, combined into a wallet verdict
